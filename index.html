Approach

I approach every project with curiosity first. Research, analytics, and behavioral observation inform my thinking, not replace it. I design with the user and the business in mind equally, and I work closely with cross-functional teams to build shared understanding around the work. I'm comfortable owning the design direction independently and bringing others along when the work is stronger for it. The goal is always the same: clarity for the user, results for the business.
