About

Bridging design and business into experiences people trust.

Curious by nature. Precise by practice.

I came to design through music. As a founding member of Five Seconds Expired, a nationally recognized band, I was doing all the creative work: logos, album artwork, posters, merchandise, photography. That foundation led me from graphic design to web design, and eventually to product design. I'm still performing and shooting today, and that creative curiosity shows up in everything I do.

That same instinct: observe, question, build, shapes how I approach every design problem.

Approach

I approach every project with curiosity first. Research, analytics, and behavioral observation inform my thinking, not replace it. I design with the user and the business in mind equally, and I work closely with cross-functional teams to build shared understanding around the work. I'm comfortable owning the design direction independently and bringing others along when the work is stronger for it. The goal is always the same: clarity for the user, results for the business.
